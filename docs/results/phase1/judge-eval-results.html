  • What “AIME” is here: AI-MO/aimo-validation-aime, 90 problems: AIME 2022, 2023 and 2024, 30 per year (AIME I and II, 15 each). Answers are integers 0–999, scored by exact match.
  • Tokens at the 4,096 limit: stock generated 106,978 tokens (1,189 per problem on average), the judge 105,089 (1,168). 81 of 90 answers ended on their own in both arms; 9 hit the limit.
  • Wall-clock on the same machine: stock 67.0 ms per token, judge 50.6 ms per token including the drafter, so 1.32× faster; 119.5 vs 88.7 GPU-minutes in total.
  • At 4,096 tokens the judge is −1.1 points [−5.6, +2.2] against stock, within noise: stock solves 7 problems, the judge 6, and 5 are solved by both.
  • Speed-up at 4,096 tokens: 1.36× fewer passes per block, 1.52× less MoE compute (judge only), 1.26× net of the drafter.
  • The longer limit barely helps: stock gains one problem (6 → 7) and the judge one (5 → 6). Only 10% of answers now reach the limit and the average answer is about 1,200 tokens, so the low score is the model's, not the budget's.
  • SDAR-30B-A3B-Chat has no published AIME score. The closest published figure is SDAR-8B-Chat at 13.8% (AIME 2024) and 10.4% (AIME 2025) with block 4, a 24K limit and 8 averaged runs (DreamReasoner-8B, arXiv 2606.19257). Our block 32, 4K limit and single greedy run are not directly comparable.

What the numbers say

  • Only IFEval is significantly below stock: −14.8 points [−18.9, −10.7]. On the other six sets every change sits inside its 95% interval, so the judge is statistically indistinguishable from stock there.
  • Net of the drafter's cost, the judge saves 1.20–1.37× compute on every set; the judge alone saves 1.42–1.60×, and it runs 1.25–1.34× fewer passes per block.
  • Blind accept is nearly free in accuracy but saves almost nothing once its drafter is counted (0.95–1.07×).
  • Against the round-3 judge, the 28.5M judge moves up on every shared set, most on MBPP (+16.7) and HumanEval (+11.6), at similar or better net speed-up.
  • IFEval's loss comes with more looping (18.1% vs 12.6% for stock) and more answers that never stop (14.4% vs 8.5%). AIME answers hit the budget without stopping 26.7% of the time.
Δ and 95% CI: paired bootstrap over the prompts both cells share, 2,000 resamples, seed 0 (the RESULTS.md method); all seven sets pair fully. Speed-ups: passes/blk = stock ÷ judge MoE passes per block; judge-only = stock exp/token ÷ judge exp/token (expert-layer loads per generated token, includes DES48 pooling, drafter ignored); judge + drafter = stock exp/token ÷ (exp/token + drafter forwards × 364.64 ÷ generated tokens), 364.64 being the 1.7B drafter in 30B-expert units.
